# Safety policy

1. Calculations, layouts and documents support engineering decisions; the
   responsible engineer checks and signs off before use.
2. Prices that are not supplied are left blank; the assistant never invents prices
   or supplier data.
3. Experience memory and outputs stay on the user's machine. Community
   contributions are anonymised and approved record-by-record by their owner.
4. PLC and IoT features (Pro edition) follow additional rules: generated PLC code is
   a draft for review and simulation, safety functions are out of scope, and IoT
   collectors are read-only.
