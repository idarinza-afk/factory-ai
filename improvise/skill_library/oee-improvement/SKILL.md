## Method
1. oee per machine/shift; identify the biggest factor (availability, performance, quality).
2. Map to the six big losses: breakdowns, setups, minor stops, reduced speed, start-up rejects, process rejects.
3. Pareto of downtime reasons; pick the top 3.
4. Actions: SMED for changeovers, root-cause (5 Why) for repeat stops, sensor/guide fixes for minor stops,
   autonomous maintenance basics, standard work.
5. Target and tracking: expected OEE gain, owner, date.
