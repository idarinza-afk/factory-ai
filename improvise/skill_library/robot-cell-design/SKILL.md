## Method
1. Task and cycle: picks per minute, product mass, gripper mass -> payload with margin.
2. Robot type: delta (fast light picks), SCARA (planar assembly), articulated 6-axis (palletising, flexible), cobot (low speed, shared space after risk assessment).
3. Reach: furthest pick/place point + gripper length + margin; check cycle time from supplier simulation.
4. Gripper: vacuum, mechanical, magnetic; product surface and hygiene.
5. Safety: fencing/light curtains/area scanners per risk assessment (ISO 10218 / ISO 12100), designed with certified safety components.
