## Method
1. Flow per line; target velocities: water 1-2 m/s, viscous products 0.5-1 m/s, pump suction < 1 m/s, CIP supply >= 1.5 m/s.
2. pipe_size -> DN; check real inside diameter of the tube standard (dairy tube, ISO, ASME).
3. Head = static + friction + equipment + required end pressure; pump_power with realistic efficiency.
4. Hygienic lines: sloped for drainage, no dead legs > 2 D, hygienic valves and fittings.
