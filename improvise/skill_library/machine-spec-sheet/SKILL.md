## Sections
1. Function and position in the line.
2. Capacity: rated speed, guaranteed output, formats/sizes, changeover time.
3. Product data: type, dimensions, temperature, packaging material.
4. Construction: materials (product contact AISI 304/316L, plastics FDA/EU compliant), surface finish, IP rating.
5. Utilities: power (kW, V/Hz/phases), compressed air (bar, NL/min), water, steam.
6. Controls: PLC/HMI brand preference, interfaces to upstream/downstream (signals), recipe handling.
7. Safety & standards: guarding, CE / ISO 12100 risk assessment, hygienic design.
8. Dimensions and weight; documentation; spare parts; warranty; FAT/SAT.

## Rules
- Mark every value as "confirmed by supplier" or "to be confirmed".
- Never fill a price or a supplier figure you do not have.
