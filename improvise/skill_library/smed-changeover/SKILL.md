## Method
1. Film/observe a changeover; list every step with time.
2. Separate internal (machine stopped) and external (can be done while running) work.
3. Convert internal to external: prepare parts, tools, materials, recipes in advance.
4. Streamline internal: quick-release fixings, change parts with locators, recipe-driven servo settings, parallel work.
5. Standardise: checklist and trolley per format; measure again; feed the gain into oee.
