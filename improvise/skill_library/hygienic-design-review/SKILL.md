## Checklist
- Materials: product contact stainless 304/316L or compliant plastics; H1 lubricants.
- Design: no dead legs, self-draining, open frames, continuous welds, no threads above product.
- Zoning: raw / cooked / packed separation; people and air flow from clean to less clean.
- Cleaning: CIP/COP/foam method defined per machine; IP rating suits the wash-down (IP65-IP69K).
- Foreign bodies: metal detector / X-ray after the last open step; sieves, magnets where relevant.
- Traceability: lot coding and verification at packing.
