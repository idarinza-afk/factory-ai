## Guidance
- Stainless 304 general; 316/316L for salt, acids, chlorides and aggressive cleaning.
- Surface finish for product contact typically Ra <= 0.8 um; welds ground and passivated.
- Plastics: food-grade with declaration of compliance (EU 10/2011 or FDA), colours that contrast with product (blue).
- Avoid wood, painted mild steel, galvanised parts and glass in product zones.
- Lubricants H1; seals and gaskets suitable for product, temperature and chemicals.
