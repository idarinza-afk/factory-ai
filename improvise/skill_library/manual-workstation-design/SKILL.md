## Method
1. Task list with times and precedence; takt from demand.
2. line_balance -> stations and efficiency; rebalance tasks above takt.
3. Ergonomics: work height (elbow height for light work), reach zones, no lifting > 25 kg (lower for frequent lifts), material presentation at the point of use.
4. Headcount with staffing; standard work sheets per station.
