## Method
1. One column per supplier; rows: capacity, compliance to spec, materials, utilities, footprint, lead time,
   warranty, references, service in country, price (as given - never estimated), payment terms.
2. Score technical items (weights agreed with the user); keep price separate.
3. Highlight deviations and missing information per supplier.
4. Save as CSV for Excel.
