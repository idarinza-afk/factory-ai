## Method
1. Product load with cooling_load (latent heat = water fraction x 334 kJ/kg when freezing).
2. Room/tunnel extra loads: transmission through walls, infiltration (door openings), fans, lights, people, defrost.
3. Design capacity = sum x margin; express in kW and TR.
4. Note temperature levels (chilled 0-4 C, frozen -18 C, blast -30 to -40 C) and refrigerant constraints.
## Output
Load breakdown table and the design capacity; equipment selection by supplier.
