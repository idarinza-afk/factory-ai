## Method
1. Baseline: utilities_summary and metered data per area.
2. Typical savings: compressed-air leaks (often 20-30 % of air), lower pressure set-point, VFDs on fans/pumps, IE3+ motors, heat recovery from compressors and refrigeration, insulation of hot surfaces, switching machines off at breaks.
3. Quantify each: kWh/year x energy price (user's price) -> roi for the investment.
4. Rank by payback; list measurement needed to confirm.
