## Method
1. Investment: machines, installation, utilities, training (prices from the user only).
2. Annual net benefit: labour saved (heads x shifts x cost), yield/quality gains, OEE gain x margin,
   energy, minus extra maintenance and running costs.
3. roi with 5-7 years and the customer's discount rate.
4. Sensitivity: rerun with benefit -20 % and investment +10 %; report payback range.

## Checks
- Every assumption listed with its source; blank where the user has not given a value.
