## Method
1. Torque and ratio with drive_torque; pick the gearbox type: helical-bevel (efficient, right angle), helical inline, worm (compact, low efficiency, self-braking tendency).
2. Chains/belts for final drive: keep speed ratio per stage <= 1:7 (chain) or 1:8 (belt); tension adjustment and guarding required.
3. Shafts: shaft_diameter with torque and bending; add keyway, then round up to standard bearing bore.
4. Couplings: flexible for misalignment; size on design torque.
## Checks
Efficiency chain: total efficiency = product of stage efficiencies; verify motor power again.
