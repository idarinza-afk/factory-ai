## Method
1. Hazards and defects from HACCP / customer specs: foreign bodies, weight, seal, label, code, shape.
2. Metal detector for metals in non-metallised packs; X-ray for metallised packs, glass, stone, bone and fill checks.
3. Checkweigher for average-quantity rules; vision for labels, codes, seals, defects.
4. Reject systems with confirmation, lockable reject bins, fail-safe design.
5. Fill weight capability: process_capability on sample weights; set target to meet legal and customer limits.
