## Method
1. Circuits: group equipment that can be cleaned together; tanks need spray devices, lines need flow.
2. Typical cycle: pre-rinse -> caustic (e.g. 1-2 %, 70-80 C) -> intermediate rinse -> acid when needed (scale) -> final rinse -> optional sanitising. Times per soil type.
3. Flow: velocity >= 1.5 m/s in the largest pipe of the circuit (pipe_size in reverse); tank spray devices per supplier flow rate.
4. Heating: heat_load for the CIP solution heat-up; pumps with pump_power.
5. Verification: conductivity, temperature and flow recorded per step; return line monitoring.
## Note
Chemical concentrations and times come from the chemical supplier and the hygiene plan.
