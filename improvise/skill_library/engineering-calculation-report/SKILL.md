## Format
1. Title, project, revision, date, prepared/checked.
2. Purpose and scope.
3. Inputs with units and source (customer, supplier, assumption).
4. Method and formulas (named, with references to standards when relevant).
5. Results table (from the tools) and conclusion.
6. Assumptions and open points; checker comments.
## Rule
Every result in the report must come from a tool call shown in the conversation.
