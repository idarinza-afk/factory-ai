## Method
1. Risk register: risk, cause, impact (cost/time/quality/safety), probability, owner, mitigation, status.
2. Typical risks: unclear URS, long lead items, site readiness, utilities not ready, product samples late, software scope creep.
3. Change requests: description, reason, impact on price/schedule/documents, approval before work; update drawings and lists with a new revision.
4. Save registers as CSV for Excel.
