# Snack seasoning, coating, packing and inspection

## Seasoning and coating
- Tumble drum: auger or vibratory feeder doses powder into a rotating flighted drum; drum angle and speed set the
  dwell time. Dry powder needs surface oil to stick: on baked (non-fried) products spray oil first.
- Electrostatic seasoning (charged powder, grounded drum): fuller coverage and 10-45 % less powder.
- Sugar coating: drizzle or curtain of hot syrup on a belt, then sesame from a vibratory feeder, then a cooling
  tunnel to set the sugar.

## Packing and inspection
- Multihead weigher + VFFS (pillow bag, nitrogen flush). Fragile products: low drop heights, gentle transfers.
- Metallized film: metal detectors cannot inspect through it well, so inspect the closed bag with X-ray (or a metal
  detector on loose product before packing); a VFFS has little room for a reject, so reject after the bagger.
- Typical order after the bagger: date coder -> checkweigher -> X-ray -> case packing (order per customer standard).
