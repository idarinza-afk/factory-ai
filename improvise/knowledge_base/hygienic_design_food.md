# Hygienic design notes for food lines

- Product contact parts: stainless steel (commonly AISI 304; 316/316L for salty,
  acidic or chloride cleaning conditions) or food-grade plastics with
  declarations of compliance.
- Surfaces cleanable and drainable: no dead legs, sloped surfaces, continuous
  welds ground smooth, open frame designs, no hollow sections that trap water.
- Separate high-care (cooked / ready-to-eat) and low-care (raw) zones; product and
  people flow one way from raw to cooked to packed.
- Plan cleaning: CIP for closed systems, COP or foam/wash-down for open equipment.
  Choose motor and electrical protection (IP65-IP69K) to match the wash-down method.
- Lubricants in product zones must be food-grade (H1).
- Consider metal detection / X-ray after the last open process step, and
  traceability (lot coding) at packing.
- Reference frameworks: EHEDG guidelines, 3-A sanitary standards, GMP and HACCP
  requirements of the customer and the local authority.
