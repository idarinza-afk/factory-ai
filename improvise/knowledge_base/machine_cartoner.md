# Cartoner

## What it does / working principle
A cartoner takes flat carton blanks or pre-glued sleeves from a magazine, erects them, loads product (bags, trays, bottles, sachets, leaflets) and closes the ends with tuck flaps or hot-melt glue. A coder or verification camera is often built in.

## Main types and when to choose each
- **Horizontal end-load (side-load):** product is pushed into the carton from the side by buckets and pushers. Suits most food packs and runs at medium to high speed. One maker quotes a range of about 10–120 cartons/min across its models (typical).
- **Vertical top-load:** product drops or is placed into the carton from above. Suits fragile, irregular or hand-loaded product, at lower speeds. One maker quotes about 25–40 cartons/min (typical).
- **Intermittent motion:** the carton stops at each station. Simpler, cheaper and easier to change over.
- **Continuous motion:** the carton moves without stopping. Higher speeds and gentler handling, but more complex.
- **Wrap-around cartoner:** the blank is wrapped around a product group, for example multipacks.
- **Closure:** tuck (reclosable, no glue) or glue (more secure, tamper-evident).

## Key specifications to ask for
- Carton style and size range (L × W × H), board grade, blank tolerance
- Speed at your format, number of formats, changeover time, tool-less change parts
- Product infeed (bucket chain, robot, pick-and-place), leaflet inserter
- Closure type, glue system, coding and verification (no carton, no product, open flap)
- Magazine capacity (minutes of autonomy)

## Sizing / selection notes
- Carton quality and consistency strongly affect efficiency. Get board samples and run trials.
- Choose continuous motion only where the rate needs it. Intermittent machines are simpler to maintain.
- Size magazine autonomy so an operator can reload it during normal rounds.

## Integration with the line
Upstream: wrappers or baggers. A collator or robot groups products and presents them to the buckets. Use "no product, no carton" logic. Downstream: case packer, checkweigher for missing-item checks. Rejection of empty or open cartons happens on the cartoner outfeed.

## Hygiene and safety notes
Usually secondary packaging, so dry cleaning is enough. Guard the pushers, the carton erector and the glue system (hot surfaces). Keep board dust under control.

## Common problems and first checks
| Symptom | Likely causes | First checks |
|---|---|---|
| Cartons not opening | Blank warp, board humidity, vacuum cups worn | Board storage, cup condition, vacuum level |
| Product jamming at insertion | Product out of bucket position, timing | Bucket and pusher phase, product size |
| Open flaps / poor glue | Glue temperature or pattern, compression time | Glue gun nozzles, temperature, compression section |
| Frequent magazine feed errors | Overloaded magazine, blank variation | Magazine pressure, blank tolerance |
