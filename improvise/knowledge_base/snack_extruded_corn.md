# Extruded corn snack (hot / cooking extruder, direct expanded)

Flow: corn grits / flour (+ rice, other cereals) -> sifter and magnet -> mixing with water to the feed moisture
-> twin-screw (or single-screw) cooking extruder, die face cutter -> pneumatic or belt transfer -> dryer / toaster
until crisp -> cooling -> oil or oil-slurry spray and seasoning in a tumble drum -> cooling -> surge bin -> packing.

Reference values (corn grit snack, extrusion study): feed moisture 16.0 +/- 0.5 %, melt temperature 156-158 C,
screw speed 300 rpm, feed 444 g/min (lab scale), expansion ratio 3.9, bulk density about 70 g/L (blend with 50 %
broken rice: expansion 3.7, 76.6 g/L). Use the bulk density to size bags, hoppers and surge bins.

Line sizes (one supplier): twin-screw lines 120-150 kg/h with 52 kW, 200-260 kg/h with 86 kW, 300-500 kg/h
with 140 kW, line length 25-32 m. Confirm final moisture after drying and dryer sizing with the supplier.
