# Tray Sealer and Thermoformer

## What it does / working principle
- **Tray sealer:** pre-formed trays, already filled with product, are indexed into a sealing tool. A lidding film is laid over them. The tool closes, and can pull a vacuum and/or flush gas (MAP) before a heated plate seals the film to the tray flange. The film is then cut.
- **Thermoformer (thermoform-fill-seal):** a bottom web from a reel is heated and formed into cavities by vacuum and/or pressure. The cavities are filled in-line, sealed with a top web (with options for vacuum, MAP or skin), and cut into packs.

## Main types and when to choose each
- **Tray sealer, semi-automatic:** low volume, many formats.
- **Tray sealer, automatic in-line:** medium to high volume with bought-in trays and stable formats.
- **Thermoformer:** high volume, custom cavity shapes, long runs. It avoids buying and shipping pre-formed trays. It suits flexible, semi-rigid or rigid films.
- **Pack options on both:** seal only, vacuum, MAP, vacuum skin pack (VSP), shrink.

## Key specifications to ask for
- Tray or cavity dimensions and impressions per cycle, cycles/min, packs/min. Example: one in-line tray sealer is quoted at up to 15 cycles/min and about 105 ppm (typical of this class).
- Changeover time (one example is about 10 min with a tooling trolley), tool cost per format
- Web widths and film structures (thermoformer). Seal temperature, pressure and dwell control.
- Gas mixing and analysis, residual O2 capability, vacuum pump capacity
- Hygiene class and washdown

## Sizing / selection notes
- Throughput = cycles/min × impressions per cycle. Vacuum and gas time often limit cycle speed, so ask for the rate *with your MAP recipe*.
- Thermoformer index length = cavity pitch × rows. Material use and web scrap (edge trim, skeleton) affect running cost.
- MAP gas mix and target residual O2 are product-specific. Confirm them with shelf-life studies.

## Integration with the line
- Upstream: portioning, a multihead or depositors over the tray or cavity.
- Downstream: leak detection on MAP packs, labelling or TTO, checkweigher and X-ray, case packing.
- The thermoformer's filling zone sets the line layout. Plan operator or robot access to it.

## Hygiene and safety notes
Open-frame stainless design is used for raw meat, fish and cheese. The tooling is a crush hazard, so guard it with interlocks. Store tooling clean and labelled to prevent swaps between formats.

## Common problems and first checks
| Symptom | Likely causes | First checks |
|---|---|---|
| Leaking seals | Product on flange, low temperature or pressure, damaged seal rubber | Flange cleanliness, seal parameters, tool silicone |
| High residual O2 | Short gas time, leaks in tool, mixer issue | Gas analyser, tool gaskets, vacuum level |
| Poor forming / thin corners | Heater temperature, web temperature, forming vacuum | Heater plates, forming time, film spec |
| Film wrinkles / misregistration | Film tension, print mark sensor | Brake, sensor teaching |
