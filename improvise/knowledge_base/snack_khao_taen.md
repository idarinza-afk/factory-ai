# Khao taen (Thai glutinous rice cracker), baked, with sugar syrup and sesame

Flow: glutinous rice -> wash -> soak overnight -> steam until sticky -> mix while hot with watermelon juice, sugar
and salt (rest about 20 min) -> form round discs (press moulds) -> dry -> puff by BAKING (hot air oven; not frying)
-> cool -> sugar syrup drizzle (palm sugar / cane sugar caramel) -> sesame sprinkle -> cooling tunnel so the sugar
sets -> packing.

- Drying: traditionally sun-dried about 2 days; in a plant use a hot-air dryer (studied at 40-60 C, air velocity
  0.5-1.5 m/s) so production does not depend on the weather.
- Puffing by hot air: a close reference (khao kob, glutinous rice sheet) puffs at 200 C for about 5 min; trial the
  real khao taen recipe to set temperature and time. Air-fryer style puffing of rice crackers has also been studied.
- Moisture/aw: the khao kob study reached 3.3-5.2 % moisture and aw 0.32-0.42; the Thai community standard limit is
  moisture <= 15 % and aw <= 0.6.
- Sugar coating is hygroscopic: control packing-room humidity, use a moisture-barrier film.
- Sesame is an allergen: cleaning between products and labelling.
